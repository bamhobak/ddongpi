Zig Zag Poop — Privacy Policy

한국어 · English · 日本語

Effective: October 15, 2026 (posted October 8, 2026)

Bamhobak Friends ("we") operates the game Zig Zag Poop (Korean title: 이피저피똥피, Japanese title: あっちこっちうんち; the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game uses, why, and how it is stored and deleted.

The Game has no sign-up and does not collect information that identifies you — no real name, phone number, email address or location. Only the nickname you choose for the leaderboard and your game records are stored on our server.

1. Information we process and why

ItemDetailsPurpose
NicknameA 2–6 character name you choose (up to 8 if letters and numbers only)Showing the leaderboard, preventing duplicate nicknames
Game recordsScore, stage reached, play time, chosen character and abilities, coins collected, game mode, time of recordLeaderboard, game balancing
Movement recordsYour character's positions during a round"Ghost" replays shown to other players
Device identifierA random value the Game creates on first launch (not your device serial number or advertising ID)Grouping your records, preventing duplicates
ProgressLevels, coins, unlocked characters and pets, settingsStored on your device
Error reportsWhen an error occurs: the error message, game version and language, device/browser type (user agent), device identifier, and game state at that moment (screen, character, stage)Finding and fixing bugs
ReportsA nickname you report from the leaderboard and the identifier of the reporting deviceRemoving offensive nicknames, preventing duplicate reports

This information is created automatically while you play or entered by you. Connection data (such as IP addresses) may briefly remain in our hosting providers' security logs.

Legal basis (EEA/UK users): we process this information because it is necessary to provide the Game's features you use (leaderboard, replays) and for our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working and fair (balancing, bug fixing, removing offensive names).

2. How long we keep it

  • Nickname, game records and movement records: until the Game is discontinued, or deleted without delay when you ask.
  • Error reports: deleted once the cause is fixed, and never kept longer than one year.
  • Reports: deleted as soon as they are handled.
  • Progress: until you clear the app's data or ask us to delete it.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with other companies or individuals, except where required by law.

If you do not want your nickname transferred, you can play without registering a nickname. Scores may still be stored without a nickname.

5. Your rights

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your nickname and records at any time. Email us with your nickname at the address below.
  • Information stored on your device can be deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.
  • If you are in the EEA or UK, you may also object to processing or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. California residents have the right to know and delete the information described above; we do not sell or share personal information.

6. Children

The Game does not collect information that identifies you, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Please do not use your real name or contact details as a nickname. Parents or guardians can make the requests above on a child's behalf.

7. Advertising

The Game currently shows no ads. If we add ads, we will update this policy first to explain what information is used.

8. Security

  • All communication with our server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The database can only be read or written through specific, limited functions.
