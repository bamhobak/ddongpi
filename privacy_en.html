Zig Zag Poop — Privacy Policy

한국어 · English · 日本語

Effective: November 8, 2026 (posted November 1, 2026; previous version: October 15, 2026)

Bamhobak Friends ("we") operates the game Zig Zag Poop (Korean title: 이피저피똥피, Japanese title: あっちこっちうんち; the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game uses, why, and how it is stored and deleted.

The Game has no sign-up and does not ask for your real name, phone number, email address or location. Everything stored on our server is listed in the table in section 1.

This information is created automatically while you play or entered by you. Connection data (such as IP addresses) may briefly remain in our hosting providers' security logs.

Legal basis (EEA/UK users): we process this information because it is necessary to provide the Game's features you use (leaderboard, replays) and for our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working and fair (balancing, bug fixing, removing offensive names).

2. How long we keep it

  • Nickname, game records and movement records: until the Game is discontinued, or deleted without delay when you ask.
  • Error reports: deleted once the cause is fixed, and never kept longer than one year.
  • Reports: deleted as soon as they are handled.
  • Progress: until you clear the app's data or ask us to delete it.
  • Device transfer code: the copy of your progress is deleted as soon as the code is used on the new device. An unused code is deleted when you get a new code on the same device.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with other companies or individuals, except where required by law.

5. Your rights

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your nickname and records at any time. Email us with your nickname at the address below.
  • Information stored on your device can be deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.
  • If you are in the EEA or UK, you may also object to processing or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. California residents have the right to know and delete the information described above; we do not sell or share personal information.

6. Children

Children under 14, or under the minimum age in their country, should play only with a parent's or guardian's consent. If we learn that we have processed such a child's personal information without consent, we will delete it without delay. Parents or guardians can make the requests above by emailing us the child's nickname. Please do not use your real name or contact details as a nickname.

7. Advertising

The Game currently shows no ads. If we add ads, we will update this policy first to explain what information is used.

8. Security

  • All communication with our server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Access rules on the database limit what can be read or written.
